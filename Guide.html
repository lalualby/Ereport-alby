Guide to Use E-Report

Welcome to the E-Report Guide! This guide will help you understand how to use the E-Report system effectively. Whether you're a student, teacher, or staff member, this guide will provide you with step-by-step instructions on how to create, manage, and submit reports.

Comprehensive Guide & Policies

  1. Accessing the Reports Menu:Open the All Reports page via the top navigation.
  2. Opening the Form Modal: Click the "Create New Report" or "Report!" button located at the top of the page.
  3. Provide Details & Title: Fill in the required information and provide a title for your report, Example: "Broken Chair Report".
  4. Select Category & Location: Choose the appropriate category and location for your report.
  5. Describe the problem: Provide a detailed description of the issue you are reporting.
  6. Submit the Report: Review your report and click the "Submit" button to finalize it.

When uploading photos for your report, please ensure they are clear and relevant to the issue you are reporting. Follow these guidelines:

  • Photos should be in JPG or PNG format.
  • File size should not exceed 5MB.
  • Include a brief description for each photo.
